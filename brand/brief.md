# Susurro — logo brief (redesign, fast track)

## The facts
- **Name**: Susurro (Spanish/Portuguese for "whisper"). Exact spelling/casing: `Susurro`.
- **What**: Offline-first voice dictation. Local STT by default, optional cloud boost. Linux (Hyprland) + Windows, tray icon, floating pill UI, CLI.
- **Audience**: developers and professionals who live in their editor; privacy-minded; low/mid-range hardware.
- **Adjectives**: robust, beautiful, quiet, precise, warm. No hype.
- **Where it must work**: app/tray icon at 16px, floating pill, installer, README/GitHub social, settings UI.

## Assumptions (stated, per fast track)
1. Palette equity stays: teal `#0F6E56`, coral `#D85A30`, neutrals `#1C1C1A` / `#F1EFE8`. Concepts shown greyscale first; colour is applied at kit stage.
2. The in-app floating pill shape language matters; the icon and the live UI should feel like one object.
3. No custom typeface; system/geometric sans (plan already decides this).
4. Decision-maker is the repo owner, and speed beats ceremony: three concepts, one checkpoint.

## Critique of the current mark (redesign §7.1)
First impression: a friendly waveform in an app tile. Instantly voice-related, warm colours.
What works: instant category read; simple geometry (audit 99/100, 54 anchors); reads at 32px.
Scorecard (abbrev): Idea 3, Simplicity 4, Distinction 2, Memorability 3, Tone 4, Craft 4, Versatility 2, Longevity 3.
Top 3 problems:
1. No standalone symbol: the mark only exists inside its tile, so no one-colour, reversed, or tray version is possible as drawn (cream bar vanishes on white).
2. The symmetric five-bar waveform is the category cliché with no twist; it reads "loud and rigid" while the name means whisper.
3. Gaps are ~24/512 units; bars will merge at 16px. No small-size cut exists.

## Equity audit (what stays / what goes)
Keep: the palette (strongest equity, already in UI), the rounded-tile app-icon shape, the waveform idea as one option.
Drop or earn: symmetric rigid bars, the unexplained cream middle bar, tile-dependence.

## Category clichés (off-limits unless given fresh form)
Microphone icons, concentric broadcast arcs, equalizer bars, chat bubbles, blue gradients, symmetric waveforms.

## Word map
whisper → quiet, breath, hush, soft, trailing off · dictation → voice, words, text, punctuation → **comma**
offline → self-contained, local, private · robust → solid, grounded, even weight.

## Longlist (11 one-liners) → shortlist (bold)
1. **Refined tile waveform: the current mark rebuilt on a grid, optically spaced, with standalone/mono/small-size versions.** (evolution)
2. Refreshed tile with asymmetric rising bars (speech captured left-to-right).
3. **Whisper S: a single-weight ribbon S whose top terminal rises into a sound bar; the initial speaks.** (letterform)
4. S built from two stacked waveform bars.
5. Lowercase s as a sound-hole / speaker aperture.
6. **Dictation comma: a bold comma, the smallest unit of written speech; "speech, written down."** (abstract/pictorial)
7. Comma fused with a waveform bar as its tail.
8. Pill mark: the floating pill itself with three bars (icon = live UI object).
9. Ripple dot: record dot emitting two right-side-only arcs (speaking, not broadcasting).
10. Waveform decaying into a dot, loud → whisper left-to-right.
11. Wordmark with the double-s drawn as a waveform ligature.
Picked 1, 3, 6: most different from each other, spanning evolution ↔ revolution, covering emblem, letterform, abstract.
